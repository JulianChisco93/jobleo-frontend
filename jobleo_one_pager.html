The problem we solve
International students miss relevant job opportunities because the Canadian job market moves faster than they can keep up.
Newcomers face language barriers, unfamiliar platforms, and an overwhelming volume of postings. Jobleo sends personalized job alerts directly to your WhatsApp, matched to your CV, in real time.

How it works
01
Upload your CV. The platform analyzes your skills, experience and job preferences.
02
We scrape daily. Fresh listings from Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, and more, every hour 24/7.
03
AI ranks the matches. Each listing is scored against your profile for relevance and fit.
04
You get a WhatsApp alert. Top matches delivered instantly, no app download needed.
Who benefits
International students entering the Canadian workforce
Recent graduates seeking their first Canadian role
Newcomers without time to monitor multiple platforms
Students with limited Canadian job-search experience
